Is this phone okay?

Hold a phone in front of your camera. The page looks for cracks, scratches and display defects and tells you if the phone looks okay. Everything runs in your browser, and no video is uploaded.

Press Start camera to begin.
Waiting for the camera

This is a small student project (YOLOv8n trained on public Roboflow datasets), not a certified inspection. On a held-out test set it caught about 92% of damaged phones and passed about 88% of undamaged ones. It misses small or faint cracks, and results are worse in dim light or with cluttered backgrounds.

Use good light, keep the phone steady, and hold it fairly close.
